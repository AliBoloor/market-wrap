## Closing assessment

Tuesday was a **broader but still rotational advance**. The S&P 500 gained 0.3% to 7,712.57, the Dow rallied 1.0%, and the Russell 2000 rose 1.7%, while the Nasdaq Composite slipped less than 0.1%. Strong earnings reactions in Equifax, Hasbro and 3M supported non-technology shares, whereas Apple, Meta and other large technology names restrained the Nasdaq. The shift from Monday's narrow AI bounce toward small caps and industrially exposed shares improved breadth, even though it did not restore leadership in technology. ([Associated Press closing report](https://apnews.com/article/b972b64127db5412a2b02c35ecf95c88); [AP index scorecard](https://apnews.com/article/0a5ea168499c6ae617c49e10bf48f9c6))

Cross-asset signals also became less defensive. VIX fell 6.1% to 17.31, the official 10-year Treasury yield eased 3 basis points to 5.12%, TLT gained 0.8%, and Brent reversed an early approach toward $112 to settle at $108.79. The continuous WTI proxy dropped 5.5%. The evidence supports a cautiously risk-on close outside mega-cap technology, but persistent 5%-plus long yields and the Nasdaq's lag keep the regime mixed rather than fully bullish. ## What mattered today

- **Earnings rewarded execution.** Equifax rose 11.3%, Hasbro 11.2% and 3M 5.8% after stronger-than-expected results. GM fell 6.8% because its full-year profit midpoint disappointed despite a quarterly beat. ([Associated Press closing report](https://apnews.com/article/b972b64127db5412a2b02c35ecf95c88))
- **Housing weakened.** September housing starts fell 5.2% to a 1.221 million annual rate and permits slipped 0.2% to 1.400 million. Census cautioned that the starts change was not statistically distinguishable from zero because the 90% confidence interval was ±11.8 percentage points. ([Census housing release](https://www.census.gov/construction/nrc/current/))
- **Contract activity deteriorated.** Pending home sales fell 7.3% from August and 11.3% from a year earlier, with declines across all four regions. NAR attributed the weakness to fast-rising mortgage rates. ([NAR pending home sales](https://www.nar.realtor/research-and-statistics/housing-statistics/pending-home-sales))
- **Oil reversed sharply.** Brent fell to $108.79 after nearing $112, helping airlines and other fuel-sensitive companies while easing one near-term inflation pressure.
