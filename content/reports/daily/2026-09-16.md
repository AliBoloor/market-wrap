## Closing assessment

Wednesday ended **risk-off after a hawkish policy reset**, although the nearly flat Nasdaq showed that the selling was not uniform. The Federal Reserve unanimously raised its target range by 25 basis points to **3.75%–4.00%**, its first increase in three years. Stocks initially held gains, then reversed as Chair Kevin Warsh emphasized persistent inflation and economic strength. The S&P 500 fell 0.4%, the Dow 1.2%, the Nasdaq Composite less than 0.1%, and the Russell 2000 0.4%. ([Federal Reserve statement](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260916a.htm); [AP market close](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-markets-fed-rate-decision-oil-e2e82957e490b7be205db6013f621c3d); [AP cash-index closes](https://apnews.com/article/wall-street-stocks-dow-nasdaq-b082e78c9b572b6b0a94b8033a0ee96b))

The message was more important than the quarter-point itself. The median FOMC participant now projects a **4.1%** federal-funds rate at year-end, up from 3.8% in June, alongside stronger 2026 growth, lower unemployment and slightly higher inflation projections. August retail sales also rose 1.2% after July's revised 0.5% decline, reinforcing the resilience case. This combination pushed the two-year yield to 4.73%, six basis points above late Tuesday, while the 10-year rose only one basis point to 5.01%. That front-end-led move and bank-stock weakness are consistent with a tighter expected policy path. ([Federal Reserve projections](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomcprojtabl20260916.htm); [AP retail-sales report](https://apnews.com/article/474ed13de492362589405a47ec044d29); [AP market close](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-markets-fed-rate-decision-oil-e2e82957e490b7be205db6013f621c3d))

Oil's retreat provided some offset, and QQQ finished fractionally higher. But SPY, QQQ and IWM all remain below their 50-day averages, the dollar broke above 100, and VIX closed higher after an intraday spike to 18.94. Thursday will test whether the post-Fed move broadens or stabilizes.
