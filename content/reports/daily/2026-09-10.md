The inflation-and-oil squeeze intensified Thursday, producing a fourth consecutive S&P 500 decline and a **risk-off close**. The S&P 500 and Dow each lost 0.6%, the Nasdaq Composite fell 0.7%, and the Russell 2000 declined 1.0%. At the same time, WTI surged more than 6%, Brent settled at $107.63, the 10-year Treasury yield climbed to 4.94%, and VIX rose 8.4% to 17.84. Falling stocks and bonds, small-cap weakness, higher oil and higher implied volatility all point to a broad adverse inflation shock rather than an isolated equity rotation. ([Associated Press market close](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-markets-oil-trump-rates-7fbc77061abd778608068d3beb1bbbaf); [Associated Press index closes](https://apnews.com/article/wall-street-stocks-dow-nasdaq-0c547c6cc3e374a2c04f78a90e35e113))

The catalyst mix was unusually coherent. August producer prices rose 0.4% in the month and 5.4% from a year earlier; final-demand energy jumped 4.2%, diesel fuel 24.1%, and transportation-and-warehousing services 2.3%. Those official figures predate part of the latest oil surge, which makes Friday's CPI important but not fully current either. AP reported U.S. gasoline near $4.28 a gallon, 34% above a year ago, and market-implied odds of a September Fed increase rising to roughly 73% from 61% a day earlier. ([BLS producer-price report](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ppi.nr0.htm); [Associated Press market close](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-markets-oil-trump-rates-7fbc77061abd778608068d3beb1bbbaf))

The damage extended beyond duration-sensitive growth stocks. Existing-home sales fell to their slowest pace in more than a year, homebuilders weakened, and Macy's declined despite beating expectations and raising its annual earnings forecast. Schwab reported that only 36% of S&P 500 constituents were above their 50-day averages, down from 47% the prior Friday. SPY finished almost exactly on its 50-day average, QQQ slipped below its own, and IWM remained nearly 3% below its 50-day trend. That leaves Friday's CPI as both a macro test and a technical confirmation point. ([Associated Press market close](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-markets-oil-trump-rates-7fbc77061abd778608068d3beb1bbbaf); [Schwab market update](https://www.schwab.com/learn/story/stock-market-update-open))

Together, those observations show heavier attention to volatility instruments as macro risk rose. They do **not** establish a simple bearish forecast: VIX calls and puts can be components of spreads, volatility hedges, overwrites, or dealer positioning, and aggregate volume does not reveal the initiating side. ([Schwab options-market update](https://www.schwab.com/learn/story/todays-options-market-update))

For that reason, this report does not convert raw put/call totals or option-versus-equity volume into a directional signal.
