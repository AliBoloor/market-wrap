## Closing assessment

Monday was a **broad risk-off session led by another technology selloff**. The S&P 500 fell **1.92% to 7,416.59**, QQQ lost **2.67%**, IWM declined **1.66%**, and every tracked sector closed lower. Technology dropped **3.24%** as Nvidia and other AI-linked companies weakened ahead of Nvidia's Tuesday results. The S&P 500 finished near its session low and only about 1% above its rising 200-day average. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-markets-ai-nvidia-oil-us-35786b8f539ec95f16ae0500f71cfcc7))

The defensive signal was confirmed by volatility and breadth, but not by rates or oil. VIX jumped **18.70% to 22.91**, while the 10-year yield eased to about **5.12%** and WTI fell **3.62%**. Lower yields and oil removed some inflation pressure, yet they did not stabilize equities. That divergence points to AI concentration and earnings risk—not simply another rate shock—as the dominant immediate stress.
