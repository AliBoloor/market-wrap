## Closing assessment

Tuesday finished **nearly flat at the index level but defensive beneath the surface**. The S&P 500 slipped less than 0.1% to 7,805.45, the Nasdaq Composite fell 0.2%, the Dow lost 0.3% and the Russell 2000 declined 0.4%. The session's more important message came from failed post-earnings rallies: Goldman Sachs and UnitedHealth each rose more than 4% initially, then both closed 0.2% lower. Micron's 3.6% fall added pressure to technology. ([Associated Press closing report](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-markets-us-tech-oil-mideast-a2edaf9ec297fb75ff2a4e245063ec08); [Associated Press index table](https://apnews.com/article/a0a577dfd12f63ee6780a56ab02b55c4))

Cross-asset trading was calmer than the equity rotation implied. The 10-year Treasury yield eased to 5.21%, TLT rose 0.4%, the dollar weakened and VIX held near 15. Oil remained elevated despite a modest Brent decline. That is not a classic risk-off configuration; it is better read as selective de-risking before Wednesday's CPI, another bank-earnings wave and the Federal Reserve's Beige Book.
