## Closing assessment

Thursday's headline indexes weakened, but the tape was broader and less risk-off than the Nasdaq suggested. The S&P 500 fell **0.30% to 7,720.39** and QQQ lost **1.42%**, while IWM rose **0.97%** and health care, financials and industrials all advanced. AP reported that Alphabet's **12.6%** record decline pulled the capitalization-weighted indexes lower even though most stocks rose. That makes the session a **mega-cap technology shock with positive rotation underneath**, not a market-wide liquidation. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/wall-street-stocks-dow-nasdaq-2c26b0b1bbc3bfab9f3f4b902172f458))

Macro evidence remained uncomfortable for duration-sensitive growth shares. BEA estimated third-quarter real GDP growth at **3.4% annualized**, up from 2.2%, while September headline PCE inflation reached **3.6% year over year** and core PCE **3.1%**. Yet Treasury yields fell, the dollar softened and TLT gained, providing cross-asset cushioning. Friday's Employment Cost Index is the next test of whether compensation pressure validates or softens the Fed's inflation concern.
