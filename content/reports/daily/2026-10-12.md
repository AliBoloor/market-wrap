## Closing assessment

Monday closed **mixed and mildly defensive beneath the headline index**. The S&P 500 slipped 0.1%, the Nasdaq Composite fell 0.4%, and the Dow gained 0.4%. SPY was nearly unchanged, but QQQ lost 0.7% and IWM fell 0.4%. AP reported that about 60% of S&P 500 constituents advanced, led by communication services, while semiconductors and other highly weighted technology shares pulled the capitalization-weighted indexes lower. That combination is rotation rather than broad liquidation. ([Associated Press closing report](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-oil-trump-inflation-a9a26b9706963afe9bdeda1b2eb1f51d))

The cross-asset message was less comfortable. Brent crude rose 1.8% to $106.65 and the dollar index gained 0.4%, while VIX rose to 15.20. Cash Treasury trading was closed, so there was no fresh official yield signal to adjudicate the inflation implications of higher oil. With major bank and health-care earnings due Tuesday and CPI on Wednesday, Monday's calm index close looks more like pre-event consolidation than a decisive risk-on signal.
