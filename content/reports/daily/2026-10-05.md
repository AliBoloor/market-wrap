## Closing assessment

Monday closed **risk-on at the index level, but still constrained by long rates**. The S&P 500 gained 0.7% and finished within 0.3% of its record, the Nasdaq Composite rose 1.1% to a record close, the Dow added 0.2%, and the Russell 2000 advanced 0.5%. Large technology and software shares drove the leadership: Nvidia and Broadcom each gained 2.1%, while PTC surged 33.5% after Schneider Electric proposed a $22.6 billion acquisition. ([Associated Press closing report](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-jobs-fed-rates-inflation-f83cf147deaf5a2361b1ae7eee74a627); [Associated Press index close table](https://apnews.com/article/wall-street-stocks-dow-nasdaq-7f89624b604f25f313502c77d4d0b010))

The macro backdrop was less comfortable than the equity close suggests. September ISM Services eased to 54.9 from 55.4, while new orders remained strong at 59.8 and employment returned narrowly to expansion at 50.1. More importantly for the inflation debate, the prices index rose to 74.0, its highest since July 2022. The 10-year Treasury yield increased to 5.31% and nearly touched 5.35%, while Brent fell 1.9% to $100.32 after trading between $100 and $103. The result was a familiar split: growth and AI shares absorbed higher yields, but the rates constraint did not disappear. ([ISM September Services PMI](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/services-pmi-at-54-9-september-2026-ism-services-pmi-report-302898421.html); [Associated Press closing report](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-jobs-fed-rates-inflation-f83cf147deaf5a2361b1ae7eee74a627))
