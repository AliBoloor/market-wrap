## Closing assessment

Thursday finished as a **broadening rebound, but not a clean risk-on session**. The S&P 500 rose 0.3% to 7,744.06, the Dow gained 1.1% and the Nasdaq Composite added only 0.1%. Small-cap proxy IWM rose 1.4%, while QQQ slipped 0.1%. That split matters: banks, insurers and consumer staples carried the advance as several AI leaders remained under pressure. Nvidia and TSMC's U.S. shares each fell 1.8%, while Marsh & McLennan, Kimberly-Clark and BNY gained between 3.2% and 4.7%. ([Associated Press closing report](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-kospi-tech-shares-e889ad7dee2b7dd959f1b8bc87366914))

The macro mix was uncomfortable. September producer prices jumped 1.3% month over month and 6.3% year over year, the fastest monthly increase since March 2022, while nominal retail sales rose 0.8%. Yet the 10-year Treasury yield fell about 10 basis points to 5.09%, TLT rallied 1.4%, gold gained 2.1% and VIX eased only modestly to 16.48 after touching 18.01. The observed combination is consistent with investors looking through an energy-heavy inflation shock while retaining protection against growth and policy uncertainty; the evidence does not support a single definitive cause for the bond rally. ([BLS PPI release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/ppi_10152026.htm); [Census retail-sales report](https://www.census.gov/retail/sales.html); [Associated Press closing report](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-kospi-tech-shares-e889ad7dee2b7dd959f1b8bc87366914))

## What mattered today

- **Pipeline inflation surprised sharply:** final-demand PPI rose 1.3% in September and 6.3% over 12 months. Energy prices rose 10.8% and gasoline 20.3%, while the measure excluding food, energy and trade services rose 0.5% for the month and 5.0% over the year. ([BLS PPI release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/ppi_10152026.htm))
- **Nominal demand remained firm:** September retail and food-services sales rose 0.8% month over month and 6.3% year over year; the July–September period was 5.5% above a year earlier. These figures are not adjusted for price changes. ([Census retail-sales report](https://www.census.gov/retail/sales.html))
- **The bond market rejected the first inflation impulse:** the 10-year yield finished near 5.09%, down from 5.19%, and TLT gained 1.4%. This is an observed reversal, not proof that inflation risk has disappeared.
- **Leadership broadened away from megacap AI:** the Dow and IWM outperformed QQQ, while financial and staples names helped offset losses in Nvidia and TSMC's U.S. shares. ([Associated Press closing report](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-kospi-tech-shares-e889ad7dee2b7dd959f1b8bc87366914))
- **Volatility stayed contained rather than collapsing:** VIX ended lower but remained above its 20- and 50-day averages, leaving the regime mixed.
