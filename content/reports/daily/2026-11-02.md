## Closing assessment

Monday was a **narrow technology-led risk-off close inside a broader rotation**, not a uniform liquidation. The S&P 500 fell **0.51% to 7,541.40** after gaining as much as 1.1% intraday, QQQ lost **1.36%**, and VIX rose **8.91% to 21.15**. Yet IWM was nearly flat, the Dow gained 0.4%, financials and industrials advanced, and AP reported that almost seven in ten S&P 500 stocks rose. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-markets-ai-earnings-oil-iran-048328e49e47ce7afe6c1fb9d9acf13d))

The session extended the market's reassessment of AI-linked valuations while macro data stayed expansionary. ISM's October Manufacturing PMI rose to **55.5**, with production at 59.0 and employment at 52.4; AP noted that the inflation component cooled. Lower oil and a softer 10-year yield helped nontechnology shares, but those cross-asset offsets were insufficient to stabilize capitalization-weighted indexes. The key consequence is a widening gap between weak technology trend structure and resilient participation elsewhere.
