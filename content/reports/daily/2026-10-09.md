## Closing assessment

Friday ended with a **constructive equity close but an unsettled macro backdrop**. The S&P 500, Dow and Nasdaq Composite each gained at least 0.6%, and the Russell 2000 rose 0.5%. Yet the drivers were not uniformly risk-on: the 10-year Treasury yield edged up to 5.24%, Brent crude finished at $104.72 after another wide swing, and preliminary consumer data showed both weaker sentiment and higher inflation expectations. The equity market absorbed those pressures, but it did not resolve them. ([Associated Press closing report](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-markets-oil-ai-bonds-5d0f953dbf96febb0690c1aef23fa8a9); [Associated Press index table](https://apnews.com/article/wall-street-stocks-dow-nasdaq-dafbd0c4037ee10e2a9e305f3cfa8e70))

Leadership was unusually event-driven. Humana jumped 11.6% on encouraging Medicare Advantage quality-rating data, while cell-tower owners rallied after SpaceX announced a spectrum purchase that could expand Starlink Mobile. The same announcement hit incumbent wireless carriers: T-Mobile fell 13.3%, AT&T 9.8% and Verizon 8.7%. That dispersion, alongside Thursday's semiconductor selloff, says the index remains resilient while single-industry repricing is becoming more violent. ([Associated Press closing report](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-markets-oil-ai-bonds-5d0f953dbf96febb0690c1aef23fa8a9))

## What mattered today

- **Consumers became more cautious:** preliminary October sentiment fell to 46.3 from 48.1, while year-ahead inflation expectations rose to 4.7% from 4.6% and long-run expectations to 3.5% from 3.4%. Buying conditions for durable goods deteriorated sharply. ([University of Michigan Surveys of Consumers](https://www.sca.isr.umich.edu/))
- **High yields did not derail equities:** the 10-year Treasury yield ended at 5.24%, two basis points above Thursday, while demand at recent auctions limited a larger rise.
- **Oil remained a headline risk:** Brent moved between roughly $102.50 and $105 before settling at $104.72, up 0.4%, as markets assessed the Iran conflict's impact on energy supply.
- **Delta affirmed demand but highlighted fuel pressure:** the airline reported adjusted EPS of $1.72 and expects December-quarter revenue to rise about 20%, while AP said it is absorbing a $6 billion annual fuel-cost increase.
