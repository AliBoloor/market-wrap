## Closing assessment

Friday was a **high-volatility, technology-led risk-off close**. The S&P 500 fell **3.70% to 6,910.39**, its seventh straight loss, and entered correction territory more than 10% below its October 6 record. The Nasdaq Composite lost **5.3%** and turned negative for the year, while the less technology-heavy Dow fell **1.9%**. Nvidia dropped 13.8%, the PHLX Semiconductor Index lost 10.7%, and other AI-infrastructure beneficiaries also fell sharply. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-anthropic-ai-chips-boom-c4c3f4878b12989347f8a930ed813dad))

This was severe index-level de-risking, but not uniform liquidation. AP reported that most stocks outside technology rose; the tracked sector set confirms the distinction, with health care nearly flat while technology fell **7.27%**. That makes the session a concentrated growth shock with broad index consequences rather than evidence that every industry was sold equally.

Cross-asset signals were defensive. VIX rose **26.20% to 35.89**, TLT gained **1.37%**, and the official 10-year Treasury yield fell 12 basis points to **4.92%**. The two-year yield fell 13 basis points to **4.63%**. Gold dropped 4.50%, however, so the haven response centered on Treasuries and volatility rather than all defensive assets.

## Global markets

The AI unwind was global. Japan's Nikkei and South Korea's Kospi each fell more than 5%, and European indexes also declined sharply.
