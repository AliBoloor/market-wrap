## Closing assessment

Thursday was an unambiguous **risk-off liquidation led by technology and the AI-capital-spending complex**. The S&P 500 fell **4.36% to 7,353.73**, QQQ lost **6.49%**, IWM fell **4.59%**, and VIX jumped **69.66% to 28.18**. AP reported that the Nasdaq Composite dropped 5.5% and entered a correction, 11.9% below last month's record, while the Dow lost 1,833 points. The move more than erased Wednesday's post-election rally. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-markets-ai-midterm-oil-trump-7ee9c212805aa5e70cf34d25aab7a36e))

The selloff was broad but not uniform. Technology fell 8.00%, industrials 4.12%, and energy 2.98%; health care and financials also declined but held above their 50- and 200-day averages. Treasury yields eased and TLT rose only modestly, while gold gained and Bitcoin fell, so cross-asset confirmation was defensive rather than a clean flight into every haven. Strong productivity data did not prevent the equity de-rating: the market's dominant concern was the durability, financing and political exposure of AI data-center investment, according to AP and Axios. That attribution is credible for leadership, but it does not establish a single cause for every stock's decline. ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/05/ai-data-center-election-backlash-concessions))
