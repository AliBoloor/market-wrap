## Closing assessment

Wednesday was a **broad post-election risk-on session with sharp internal rotation**. The S&P 500 rose **1.58% to 7,688.62**, IWM gained 1.51%, and VIX collapsed 19.97% to 16.67. AP reported a 1.4% Nasdaq Composite gain and a Dow advance of more than 1,500 points after the midterm result ended one-party control of Washington. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/4d12764c0b3ce38ffde3077712811182))

The headline rally concealed a major sector split: financials and health care surged more than 3%, while energy fell 4.72% as WTI dropped 7.87%; technology was essentially flat despite QQQ's 0.97% rise. Treasury yields fell, TLT rose and gold gained, confirming a decline in immediate inflation and event-risk pressure. ISM Services remained expansionary at **54.5**, but its Prices Index stayed elevated at 73.0. The result is a stronger index tape without a clean return to technology leadership.
