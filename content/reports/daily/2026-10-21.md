## Closing assessment

Wednesday was a **technology-led risk-on session**, but not an indiscriminate one. The S&P 500 gained 0.6% to 7,757.17, the Dow rose 0.7%, and the Nasdaq Composite climbed 1.1%, while the Russell 2000 added only 0.3%. SpaceX's stronger-than-expected profit and disclosure that it had taken a step toward considering a Tesla combination lifted SpaceX 7.8% and Tesla 3.4%. Nvidia added 2.5%, and Super Micro Computer rallied 9.5% after raising its summer revenue expectation. The result was stronger large-cap growth leadership and a close within 0.8% of the S&P 500's record. ([Associated Press closing report](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-markets-rates-oil-earnings-ai-4fa93685cd49e51e7ff0d840fdd38b0d); [AP index scorecard](https://apnews.com/article/3ec2d8c93a390023ef8104973dea275f))

The session still contained clear selectivity. Netflix fell 5.3% as its fourth-quarter revenue-growth outlook disappointed, and AT&T lost 6.4% despite quarterly profit beats. VIX fell 3.6% to 16.68 and Bitcoin gained 1.9%, supporting the risk-on reading, but the official 10-year Treasury yield held near 5.12% and WTI remained near $98 despite another sharp decline in the continuous contract. The close was constructive for equities, though high rates and earnings-sensitive dispersion argue against treating it as a broad all-clear.
