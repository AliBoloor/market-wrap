## Closing assessment

Friday delivered a **narrow stabilization, not a broad repair**. The S&P 500 rose **0.31% to 7,376.86**, QQQ rebounded **1.40%**, IWM gained **0.98%**, and VIX fell **9.37% to 25.54**. AP reported a 1.1% Nasdaq Composite advance but a 0.5% Dow decline, with more S&P 500 constituents falling than rising. The S&P 500 still lost 2.7% for the week—its worst week since April 2025. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/markets-japan-nikkei-oil-ai-bba6f0bf9035a6e6b1c4fef3e8230b89))

Technology led the rebound after Thursday's historic selloff, but financials, health care and energy did not participate. SPX and SPY held just above their rising 200-day averages, while QQQ, IWM, XLK and XLI remained below theirs. Treasury yields firmed after a stronger employment report, TLT was flat, and gold and Bitcoin slipped. The session therefore reduced immediate liquidation pressure without restoring broad risk-on confirmation.

## Global markets

The U.S. stabilization followed a severe global repricing: AP reported roughly 6% declines in both Tokyo and Seoul and a 1.9% fall in Germany's DAX. Those markets were reacting to Thursday's U.S. AI-led selloff, so Friday's late U.S. bounce arrived after overseas cash markets had already absorbed the shock. Global confirmation therefore remains risk-off even though U.S. technology stopped falling. ## What mattered today

- October nonfarm payrolls rose **165,000**, the unemployment rate held at **4.1%**, and prior August–September payrolls were revised up by a combined **84,000**. Average hourly earnings rose 0.4% month over month and 3.1% year over year. ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_11062026.htm))
- Labor-market detail was not uniformly strong: participation held at 61.9%, while people working part time for economic reasons increased by 339,000 to 4.8 million. ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_11062026.htm))
- AP attributed the rebound's strongest lift to AI-linked names including Nvidia, Lam Research, CoreWeave and Micron. That recovered only part of Thursday's losses and did not produce positive breadth. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/markets-japan-nikkei-oil-ai-bba6f0bf9035a6e6b1c4fef3e8230b89))
- Firmer Treasury yields after payrolls were consistent with reduced near-term recession concern, but the equity response stayed concentrated.
