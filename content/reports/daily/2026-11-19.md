## Closing assessment

Thursday was a **broad risk-off reversal**. The S&P 500 fell **2.02% to 7,175.83**, its sixth consecutive decline and longest losing streak since 2024, after trading as high as 7,415.64. QQQ lost **2.19%**, IWM fell **2.18%**, and more than 90% of S&P 500 members declined. The index closed near its session low, 8.2% below last month's record and well below its rising 200-day average. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-markets-ai-us-anthropic-206c84045137824feabe052a29ff6140))

The failed morning rally was the defining feature. Anthropic's strong IPO initially lifted AI enthusiasm and SPX gained as much as 1.3%, but Anthropic pared its advance, Nvidia reversed a 4.7% gain into a 3.3% loss, and CoreWeave swung from +10.5% to -6.1%. The breadth and closing location support a genuine de-risking interpretation rather than a sector rotation.

Cross-asset confirmation was defensive but not disorderly: VIX jumped **15.80% to 28.44**, the 10-year yield eased to roughly **5.04%–5.05%**, TLT gained **0.16%**, and gold futures fell **2.40%**. A slightly stronger dollar and weak precious metals show that the safety bid concentrated in Treasuries and cash volatility rather than every traditional haven.
