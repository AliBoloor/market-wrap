## Closing assessment

Friday extended Thursday's decline, but the character changed. The S&P 500 fell **0.52% to 7,623.68**, the Nasdaq Composite slipped **0.2%**, and the Dow lost roughly **1.1%**. Three quarters of S&P 500 constituents declined, so this was a broader risk-off session than Thursday's concentrated technology selloff. Energy was the exception: WTI rose 4.0% and XLE gained 2.6% as oil reached its highest level since June 2022. ([Associated Press close](https://apnews.com/article/7b47ef3b037bfba4e34f685329b9b963); [AP index scorecard](https://apnews.com/article/e07c72bc5cbe5dffe352ddf30054e35f))

The cross-asset message was an **inflation-sensitive risk-off close**. VIX rose to 19.59, the 10-year Treasury yield moved back toward 4.99%, TLT fell, and the dollar firmed. Technology stabilized—QQQ was nearly flat and XLK gained 0.5%—but financials, industrials, health care and small caps fell. The evidence supports oil pressure and continued concern about expensive AI investment as important context; it does not establish a single cause for every asset move.
