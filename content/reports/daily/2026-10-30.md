## Closing assessment

Friday turned Thursday's concentrated mega-cap weakness into a broader **risk-off close**, although it was still not indiscriminate. The S&P 500 fell **1.82% to 7,580.07**, QQQ lost **3.47%**, IWM declined **1.63%** and VIX rose **13.08% to 19.45**. AP reported that Intel plunged 28.6% after weak guidance and that Amazon's capital spending and cash flow intensified concern about the returns on AI investment. The Nasdaq Composite fell 2.9%, and the S&P 500 posted its worst session since June. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-ai-alphabet-oil-16c266731fdf64178f38070af2b42497))

Cross-asset confirmation was mixed but defensive. The 10-year yield proxy fell to **4.93%**, TLT gained **0.43%**, and the dollar eased, consistent with demand for duration and liquidity. Yet WTI rose and gold fell sharply, so this was not a uniform haven bid. Health care and energy finished higher while technology fell almost 5%; nearly as many S&P 500 constituents rose as fell according to AP. The best description is **technology-led risk reduction with broader index damage, buffered by defensives and Treasuries**.
