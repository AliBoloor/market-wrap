## Closing assessment

Monday delivered an **extreme technology-led relief rally**, not yet a complete technical repair. The S&P 500 rose **4.73% to 7,237.17**, recovering just over half of last week's plunge and ending a seven-session losing streak. QQQ surged **7.00%**, IWM gained **3.56%**, and the Dow advanced about 3%. Nvidia's 31.5% jump after Jensen Huang's surprise return as CEO drove a powerful rebound across AI-linked shares. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-markets-rates-technology-ai-anthropic-49ba1beba78fa4ed2bd5898029f79c91))

The session was risk-on, but unusually concentrated. XLK gained **8.97%**, more than seven percentage points ahead of health care, while energy fell. SPX remained below its 200-day average and QQQ remained almost 5% below its own. The evidence therefore supports a sharp repricing of Friday's panic rather than proof that the correction has ended.

Cross-asset confirmation was mixed. VIX collapsed **25.83% to 26.62**, TLT added **0.29%**, and official two- and ten-year Treasury yields were unchanged at **4.63%** and **4.92%**. The dollar rose 0.49%, gold rebounded 3.42%, and WTI fell 4.36%. Equity risk appetite improved without a parallel selloff in long-duration Treasuries.
