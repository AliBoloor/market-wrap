## Closing assessment

Wednesday produced a **mixed but still fragile close**. The S&P 500 fell **0.23% to 7,323.63**, its fifth consecutive decline, even as QQQ gained **0.39%** and IWM rose **0.40%**. Nvidia's post-earnings strength supported technology, but the rescue was narrow: energy fell **3.27%**, health care lost **2.20%**, and financials dropped **1.14%**. The S&P finished below both its 7,346.55 rising 200-day average and Tuesday's close. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-ai-earnings-oil-apec-shenzhen-dad5715bc91b4aba23a567a40e22b1fc))

Cross-asset signals were defensive rather than panicked. VIX eased **3.53% to 24.56** but remained well above its 20-day average. The 10-year yield fell about four basis points to **5.06%**, TLT gained **0.84%**, gold futures lost **3.48%**, and the dollar rose **0.30%**. The interpretation is a selective technology rebound inside a broader risk-off trend, not a confirmed market-wide reversal.

The October FOMC minutes added a hawkish policy constraint: almost all participants expected further tightening would likely be appropriate, with some officials discussing a larger move. Because the record predates later data, it is evidence of the committee's October debate rather than a live December decision. ([Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomcminutes20261028.htm), [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/9e72f3b6e3484173471cc382d6918de0))
