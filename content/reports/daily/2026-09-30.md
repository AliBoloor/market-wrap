## Closing assessment

September ended **mixed, narrow and still constrained by long-term yields**. The cash S&P 500 fell 0.3%, the Dow lost 0.9%, and the Russell 2000 declined 0.4%, while the Nasdaq Composite gained 0.2%. ETF proxies were consistent: SPY fell 0.21%, IWM 0.40%, and QQQ gained 0.25%. The 10-year yield rose to about 5.29%, TLT lost 0.58%, and VIX increased 1.87% to 16.34. This was not a disorderly risk-off close, but the breadth and rates combination was defensive. ([Associated Press closing report](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-markets-oil-bonds-us-ai-chips-e61d3d7b947a390c7eee6c8758e3095d); [Associated Press index close table](https://apnews.com/article/wall-street-stocks-dow-nasdaq-1ee73f14dc23f6139eeb12c7397f543e))

August PCE inflation came in at 3.4% year over year, below the 3.7% consensus cited by AP. Two-year yields eased and traders reduced the implied chance of an October rate increase, but long yields reversed higher after data suggested stronger spring growth. The split matters: softer inflation relieved the immediate policy path, while resilient growth, fiscal supply and term-premium concerns continued to pressure longer-duration assets. That interpretation is supported by the curve response; it is not a claim that any single factor explains the entire move.
