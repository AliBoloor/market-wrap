## Closing assessment

The oil shock broadened into a clearer **risk-off session** Wednesday. The S&P 500 lost 0.5%, the Dow fell 0.8%, the Nasdaq Composite declined 0.6%, and the Russell 2000 dropped 1.3%. Brent rose above $100 for the first time since July after another escalation in the U.S.–Iran war, while every S&P 500 sector except energy declined. Small-cap underperformance, weaker long-duration Treasuries and a 4.7% rise in VIX supplied cross-asset confirmation that the pressure extended beyond a handful of stocks. ([Associated Press market close](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-inflation-currencies-fed-rates-d1284eb72934a3b076c14449bc087fbd); [Associated Press index closes](https://apnews.com/article/wall-street-stocks-dow-nasdaq-31c966aef214740b8fec71e399a051b8))

The central transmission channel was energy into inflation and rates. AP reported Brent at $100.87, U.S. gasoline 32% above a year earlier, and diesel at $5.94 a gallon. The 10-year Treasury yield touched 4.85% before easing to 4.83%, while the two-year rose to 4.41% from 4.39%. Reuters also attributed part of the long-yield move to disappointment that Treasury's announced long-bond buyback, up to $6 billion, was smaller than some analysts expected. Those observations support the day's oil-and-rates explanation; the exact contribution of each channel cannot be separated from closing prices alone. ([Associated Press market close](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-inflation-currencies-fed-rates-d1284eb72934a3b076c14449bc087fbd); [Reuters market close](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/s-p-500-ends-down-as-oil-tops-100-per-barrel-ce785bd9d08ff52c))

Market breadth deteriorated, but this was not disorderly stress. VIX ended at 16.46—above its 20-day average but below the recent 16.82 high—and QQQ lost only 0.29%. Meta rose 6.3% after launching its Muse personal AI agent, while energy shares advanced; Exxon Mobil gained 2.2% and Chevron 1.9%. Retailers were notably weak, with Amazon down 1.8%, Starbucks 1.9%, and Home Depot 1%. The practical conclusion is a defensive rotation driven by an adverse nominal-growth mix, not a capitulation signal. ([Associated Press market close](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-inflation-currencies-fed-rates-d1284eb72934a3b076c14449bc087fbd))

Thursday's PPI now matters more because it arrives after oil has crossed a psychologically and economically important threshold. The release cannot yet capture all of the latest energy escalation, but it will shape how investors interpret the pre-existing inflation trend and the roughly 62% reported probability of a September Fed hike. Friday's CPI remains the more direct household-inflation test.
