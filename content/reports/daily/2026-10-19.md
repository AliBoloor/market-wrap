## Closing assessment

Monday produced a **narrow, technology-led rebound rather than a clean return to risk-on conditions**. The S&P 500 gained 0.4% to 7,690.73 and the Nasdaq Composite rose 1.1% to 26,430.89, but the Dow slipped 0.1% and the majority of U.S. stocks outside AI and technology declined. Nvidia gained 1.4%, helping the capitalization-weighted indexes recover part of last week's damage. The Russell 2000 added only 0.3%. The decline in implied volatility supports stabilization; rising yields, weak breadth and overseas stress argue against calling the session broadly risk-on.
