## Closing assessment

Monday was a **broad risk-on rebound, but not yet a full technical repair**. The S&P 500 rose **2.35% to 7,549.94**, QQQ gained **3.54%**, IWM added **3.23%**, and VIX fell **22.31% to 19.92**. AP described the S&P 500's best day since April and reported a 2.2% Dow gain and 2.7% Nasdaq Composite rally. The move recovered most of last week's damage, while a sharp oil decline reduced an important inflation and geopolitical pressure. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-markets-ai-trump-oil-d938f8e6ff42b3706e1d36e6245ec56b))

Participation improved materially: all five tracked sectors rose, led by industrials and technology. Yet SPX, SPY and QQQ remained below their falling 20- and 50-day averages, and QQQ finished just below its 200-day average. The rebound therefore changed the immediate tone from liquidation to recovery, but Tuesday's CPI and the 10-year Treasury auction are required confirmation tests.
