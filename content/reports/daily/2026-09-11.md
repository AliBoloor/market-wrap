## Closing assessment

Friday delivered a **relief rally with incomplete risk-on confirmation**. The S&P 500 gained 0.9% to break a four-session losing streak, the Dow rose 1.0%, and the Nasdaq Composite added 1.0%. Brent's 2.8% retreat to $104.61 reduced the immediate inflation impulse, while August CPI landed close to the market's headline expectation. VIX fell 11.2% to 15.84 and QQQ recovered above its 50-day average. Those are constructive signals, but the two-year yield rose eight basis points to 4.64%, the 10-year reached 4.97%, and small caps gained only 0.4%. The session therefore eased acute risk, without reversing the week's oil-and-rates tightening. ([Associated Press market close](https://apnews.com/article/financial-markets-stocks-oil-iran-war-bonds-rates-8c3272812f5e9b9238c6a3301921c17a); [Associated Press index closes](https://apnews.com/article/wall-street-stocks-dow-nasdaq-67a463295d9ea178d7802ca4338a6eb5))

Inflation did not supply an all-clear. Headline CPI rose 0.4% in August and 3.4% from a year earlier. Core CPI increased 0.3% in the month—one tenth above the consensus cited by Schwab—while easing to 2.4% year over year. Gasoline rose 3.9% in August and energy prices were 16.3% above a year earlier, even before the latest surge in crude. AP reported that stronger expectations for a rate increase next week pushed policy-sensitive yields higher. The market's positive response is best read as relief that inflation was not worse, not confidence that inflation is defeated. ([BLS Consumer Price Index](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm); [Schwab options-market update](https://www.schwab.com/learn/story/todays-options-market-update); [Associated Press market close](https://apnews.com/article/financial-markets-stocks-oil-iran-war-bonds-rates-8c3272812f5e9b9238c6a3301921c17a))

The rebound also had limits beneath the indexes. The Russell 2000 rose just 0.4% and remained 2.5% below its 50-day average. Preliminary University of Michigan data showed consumer sentiment weakening and one-year inflation expectations rising to 4.6% from 4.0%, according to AP. Kroger gained 2.5% after an earnings beat, while Oracle's initial 8.5% jump faded toward a loss. That mix reinforces a selective rebound rather than a broad reset in growth and inflation expectations. ([Associated Press market close](https://apnews.com/article/financial-markets-stocks-oil-iran-war-bonds-rates-8c3272812f5e9b9238c6a3301921c17a))
