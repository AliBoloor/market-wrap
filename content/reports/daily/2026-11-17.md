## Closing assessment

Tuesday was another **broad risk-off session**, although the close was less severe than the intraday washout. The S&P 500 fell **1.03% to 7,340.53**, QQQ lost **0.73%**, IWM declined **1.43%**, and nearly nine in ten S&P 500 members fell. SPX finished four points below its rising 200-day average after trading as low as 7,288.34. Financials were the weakest tracked sector, while technology recovered well off its low but still closed down. ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-ai-earnings-oil-4d5a35288288389d894e63e6bebe8240))

The defensive confirmation was clear: VIX rose **11.13% to 25.46**, Treasury yields eased as investors sought safety, and global equities declined. The 10-year yield finished near **5.10%**, while TLT gained only **0.21%**. This was not a clean duration-led relief trade; it was a de-risking session centered on AI uncertainty and broader economic sensitivity.

After the cash close, Nvidia reported fiscal-third-quarter revenue of **$115.8 billion**, up 103% from a year earlier, and non-GAAP diluted EPS of **$2.68**. Its fourth-quarter outlook calls for **$130.0 billion, plus or minus 3%**, with no China data-center compute revenue assumed. Those results arrived after the market observations below and are tomorrow's primary opening catalyst.
