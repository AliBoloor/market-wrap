## Closing assessment

Wednesday was a **rates-driven risk-off session**, with the pressure concentrated in long-duration technology and smaller companies. The S&P 500 fell 1.1% to 7,718.94, its worst day since July; the Nasdaq Composite lost 1.9%, the Russell 2000 fell 1.9%, and the Dow slipped only 0.1%. September CPI accelerated to 0.6% month over month and 3.7% year over year. Core CPI rose 0.3% for a second month, versus the 0.2% consensus cited by Schwab. That combination reopened the near-term rate-hike debate and punished the market's most rate-sensitive leadership. ([BLS CPI release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_10142026.htm); [Associated Press closing report](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-earnings-bonds-artificial-intelligence-704d3190f0fba2908df07c85f26346ec); [Schwab options-market update](https://www.schwab.com/learn/story/todays-options-market-update))

The close was less disorderly than the headline selloff suggests. The 10-year yield briefly exceeded 5.32% after CPI but finished near 5.19%; TLT reversed an opening decline to gain 0.7%. VIX rose 10.4% to 16.86, elevated but not at a stress level. The dollar strengthened, oil rose and gold fell. The combined signal is a repricing of inflation and duration—not a generalized flight from every risk asset—but the break below short-term support in SPY, QQQ and IWM makes Thursday's confirmation important.

## What mattered today

- **Inflation broadened beyond gasoline:** headline CPI rose 0.6%, with gasoline up 9.1% and energy up 5.2%, but shelter also rose 0.4% and core CPI increased 0.3%. Core inflation was 2.6% year over year. ([BLS CPI release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_10142026.htm))
- **Rates made a full intraday round trip:** the 10-year yield moved from below 5.17% before CPI to above 5.32%, then finished near 5.19%. Short yields rose more sharply, consistent with higher policy-rate expectations.
- **AI leadership broke lower:** Nvidia fell 3.2%, Broadcom 3.1%, AMD 5.7%, Oracle 8.8% and CoreWeave 15.5%, according to AP. Higher discount rates and financing costs are the direct market concern for capital-intensive AI investment.
- **Earnings misses were punished:** Bank of America fell 4.8% after weaker-than-expected profit and revenue, while State Street lost 4.1% after a revenue miss.
- **The Dow's resilience was narrow, not risk-on:** its 0.1% decline contrasted with the Nasdaq and Russell 2000, showing rotation away from duration rather than broad confidence.
