## Week in review

The week was a **broad relief rally with a still-fragile technology and inflation backdrop**. The S&P 500 cash index gained **2.50%**, the Dow rose **2.07%**, the Nasdaq Composite added **2.27%**, and the Russell 2000 advanced **1.44%**. All eleven tracked sector ETFs rose, VIX fell 6.84 points to 18.78, and Monday's 2.3% SPX jump supplied most of the weekly advance. Breadth and lower implied volatility therefore improved materially from the prior week's shock.

The path was less reassuring than the headline. Monday's oil-led relief rally and Wednesday's AI rebound were interrupted by Tuesday's hotter CPI, then by Thursday's Nvidia leadership change and Friday's renewed semiconductor weakness. SPX still closed below its 20- and 50-day averages, while QQQ finished just under its 200-day average. The evidence supports a **rotation-heavy repair rather than a restored momentum regime**. ([Monday close](https://apnews.com/article/f7ec9680fc858e3c2505913e7b3a54e2); [Tuesday inflation reaction](https://apnews.com/article/ffe205fbc4fddf65c3e8e64e740355b7); [Wednesday AI rebound](https://apnews.com/article/297d040c83844a9563327340b370c24e); [Thursday close](https://apnews.com/article/3f3668a937a11535103e3a5c8d82c9fc); [Friday context](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-markets-ai-us-oil-f8edd0eeb1494d4d2584715686c5e619))

Cross-asset confirmation was mixed. The Dollar Index rose 1.09%, the Yahoo 10-year yield index rose about 30 basis points, TLT fell 3.10%, LQD lost 1.63%, gold fell 4.73%, and Bitcoin dropped 9.45%. HYG slipped only 0.39%, and equities held most of the rebound despite higher rates. That combination is inconsistent with a clean risk-on signal: equity breadth improved, but duration, investment-grade bond prices, gold and crypto absorbed a substantial inflation/rates repricing.

## Macro, policy and earnings pulse

- **Inflation reasserted control of the rates narrative.** October CPI rose 0.4% month over month, with shelter up 0.3%, food up 0.5%, energy up 0.7%, and core CPI up 0.3%. BLS could not publish most October year-over-year comparisons because October 2025 survey data were unavailable during the appropriations lapse. The release supports a sticky-inflation interpretation without inventing a missing annual rate. ([BLS CPI](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_11102026.htm))
- **Producer prices cooled sequentially but remained elevated.** Final-demand PPI rose 0.3% in October and 6.5% over twelve months; final demand excluding food, energy and trade services rose 0.5% on the month and 4.9% over the year. Truck freight prices rose 3.9%, an important sign of transportation-cost pass-through. ([BLS PPI](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ppi.nr0.htm))
- **Policy expectations tightened.** Cboe's Tuesday commentary reported a 90.5% market-implied probability of a 25-basis-point December hike and the appearance of a 9.5% probability of a 50-basis-point move. Those are dated market probabilities, not forecasts. The subsequent rise in two- and ten-year yields and decline in TLT confirm that investors repriced the policy path. ([Cboe CPI reaction](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/hot-cpi-report-puts-fire-under-interest-rate-hike-possibilities))
- **Enterprise and semiconductor demand remained strong, but governance risk disrupted the AI complex.** Cisco reported record $18.5 billion quarterly revenue, up 24% year over year, and raised fiscal-year guidance. Applied Materials reported record $10.41 billion quarterly revenue, up 53%. Those results argue against an immediate collapse in AI/networking investment. Nvidia's board separately announced Jensen Huang's immediate resignation and Colette Kress as interim CEO; that company-specific governance shock drove late-week concentration risk and cannot be generalized to the whole economy. ([Cisco results](https://newsroom.cisco.com/c/r/newsroom/en/us/a/y2026/m11/cisco-reports-first-quarter-earnings.html); [Applied Materials results](https://investor.appliedmaterials.com/news-releases/news-release-details/applied-materials-announces-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2026); [Nvidia transition](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition/))
