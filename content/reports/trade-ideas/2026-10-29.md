## How to use the three maps

Each ticker has a distinct next-session, week and month technical corridor. Short
puts sit at or below mapped support; short calls sit at or above mapped resistance;
long puts are five points lower and long calls five points higher. These are
rounded technical strike hypotheses. **You choose expiries; no expiry is selected
or implied by the technical horizon.** Longer-horizon levels are not the same
next-day bounds copied forward, and a wide corridor does not imply an attractive
credit or a high win probability.

## Shared schematic and risk notes

The curve shows signed intrinsic shape before entry cash flow, shifted vertically
by the unknown credit and costs. It is an expiry-payoff schematic for whatever
same-expiry package you select—not a forecast of P&L on the technical horizon.
Pre-expiry value also depends on remaining time and implied volatility. No net
profit, exact breakeven or executable premium is invented. You select expiries;
all proposed technical strikes require a listing/deliverable/liquidity check.

With five-point wings, one 100-multiplier intact package has modeled maximum
expiry loss **500 − 100C + F**, where C is net credit in points and F is actual
all-in USD costs. The **$500 conditional entry requirement** needs 0<C<5 and
100C≥F. Unknown credit/fees means eligibility is unconfirmed, not a guaranteed
account-loss limit. ETF assignment/pin/after-hours residual-stock risk can exceed
the intact-package model; execute/manage the complete package. SPX requires
exact AM/PM settlement and contract verification. No naked legging or broker orders.

## Evidence and methodology

Saved two-year daily OHLC is clipped through October 29. Daily-bar timestamps
identify the session opening bar; the selected OHLC was retrieved after the
completed cash close. The baseline as-of is 16:00 New York, not the daily bar's
09:30 timestamp. No October 30 observations or later close is used.

Methods combine daily/5/10/20/50/60/200/252-session ranges; 20/50/200-session simple
averages and slopes; and confirmed swing pivots requiring two bars on each side
within the saved history. The 20-session range is a month proxy, not a calendar
month return. 38.2% and 61.8% retracements of that observed range are secondary
confluence aids, not independently predictive levels. Calculations and pivot
dates remain in the inspectable technical JSON. ETF volume is compared with the
20-session mean; SPX cash volume is unavailable. Ticker-specific next-day versus
weekly/monthly anchors are explained in every row.

Public [SquawkFlow methodology and dated observations](https://squawkflow.com/gex)
provide the qualified SPX gamma context. This is an assumed-position provider
model, not an audited dealer book; its front-expiry levels were withdrawn. The
other four tickers have explicit gamma gaps. No inaccessible posts, private
feeds, performance record or open-interest-derived dealer facts are invented.
